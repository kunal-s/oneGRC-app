# Workflows

Generated by Prototype to Product Kit v2.1 · 2026-08-27
Last updated: 2026-08-27

When someone does this, what happens. One row per transition, carrying both the
functional fact and its technical consequence.

This document passed three entities and four hundred lines, so it is split per
module under `docs/plan/workflows/`. Each file is named for the module's dominant
entity and carries every state machine in that module, because a builder opens a
module, not an entity. This file is the index and the cross-entity content.

**`In prototype` values used throughout.**

| Value | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `REAL` | The transition happens and its consequence is visible |
| `SIMULATED` | The label changes and nothing else happens |
| `DEAD END` | The control exists and leads nowhere |
| `NOT PRESENT` | The FRD requires it and the prototype has no trace of it |
| `LIVE` | Built through `apps/api` and persisted |

## 4. Journeys

The end-to-end flows the FRD describes, as numbered steps. Each step names who
acts, what they are doing in the customer's language, and what changes.

### JRN-01 A law becomes a tracked, evidenced duty

The spine. FRD §5.1, §5.2, §5.4, §5.6.

1. A Compliance Analyst supplies an instrument's name and its source URL, or
   uploads the document. The document is stored by its content hash, so the firm
   can later prove an extracted clause came from that exact file.
2. The extractor breaks the instrument into provisions and classifies each one.
   Nothing is tracked yet, and every extracted figure is marked unverified.
3. The system raises a flag on every provision that needs a human eye: a cadence
   the instrument defers elsewhere, an applicability that turns on a fact about
   the firm, an unresolved cross-reference, an amended text, a proviso, a
   discretion, or low extraction confidence.
4. The Head of Compliance opens one provision, reads the verbatim text, and
   resolves its blocking flags. Resolving the cadence flag means linking the
   provision in the subordinate Rules that supplies the answer.
5. The Head of Compliance decides. Promoting it mints a tracked clause with a
   citable identifier, frozen against the words in force. The alternatives are
   engaging an external specialist, or marking it not applicable with a recorded
   basis. All three are decisions and all three are logged.
6. The Head of Compliance attaches the clause to an existing control, or creates
   a control from it. The control now lists the clause among those it satisfies,
   grouped by act.
7. The Head of Compliance creates the duty the control discharges, with a
   cadence, an owner, a nominated checker and the evidence the statute itself
   requires. The first cycle is scheduled with its due date.
8. The system chases the cycle on the ladder at seven, three and one days before
   the due date.
9. The owner does the work, attaches the treasury challan to the task and
   submits. Submission is refused with nothing attached, because the statute
   requires proof of payment.
10. The nominated checker verifies the artifact against the period and the
    amount, and approves. The task reads Verified, the cycle reads Filed, and
    the evidence reads Verified.
11. The system schedules next month's cycle, and records this one as on time or
    late.
12. Anyone can now run the chain in both directions: from the duty back to the
    exact provision and its citation, and from the provision forward to the
    control, the duty, the task and the proof.

### JRN-02 A monitored control fails and three regulators must be told

The cascade. FRD §5.9, §5.10, §5.22, §5.11.

1. A monitoring rule runs against the vulnerability feed and finds three
   critical items past the fourteen-day window.
2. The run captures itself as evidence and the rule reads Failing. The control's
   result becomes Fail.
3. The system raises one remediation issue naming those three items. Nobody had
   to notice.
4. The failure has already produced a real event, so the issue is linked to the
   live incident.
5. The incident's shape engages three regulator tracks: cyber-enabled,
   subscriber-impacting and personal data involved. Every clock starts at
   detection, not at the time someone opened the record.
6. The responder works one timeline: containment, eradication, recovery, each
   entry attributed and timestamped.
7. The responder drafts each regulator's report from the one timeline and the
   one evidence set, in that regulator's format.
8. A second person files each track. The acknowledgement is captured as
   evidence.
9. The responder books the loss on the standard categories. Net is derived from
   gross minus recovery, and cannot be keyed.
10. The Executive closes the incident. Closure is refused while a required track
    is unfiled.
11. The three filings all cite the same artifacts, gathered once.

### JRN-03 An assessment cycle moves the register

FRD §5.16.

1. The Risk Manager opens an assessment campaign scoped to the operational risk
   domain, with a due date.
2. The system creates one task per in-scope risk and fans them into each owner's
   queue, chasing on the ladder against the campaign due date.
3. A first-line owner confirms the risk is still relevant, re-scores likelihood
   and impact, rates every mitigating control, proposes a treatment and records
   the reasoning. The residual as it stood when the assessment was drafted is
   stamped on the response.
4. The checker challenges. Where the evidence does not support the score, the
   task is returned with a reason and the owner reworks it.
5. On approval the new score is written back to the risk, with a timeline entry
   recording what changed.
6. The heat map, the domain aggregate, the appetite status and the exposure
   trend all move, because all four read the register.
7. The Risk Manager closes the campaign once every task is resolved, and the
   completion certificate is filed as evidence against the assessment duty.

### JRN-04 A committee pack becomes a view rather than a project

FRD §5.26.

1. The Head of Compliance chooses the audience, the Audit Committee, and the
   period.
2. The system offers the sections that audience takes. Each section is a live
   query over the register. A section whose module is not built is absent, not
   empty.
3. The pack names the basis it is produced under, and says plainly where that
   basis is best practice rather than a binding duty.
4. The Head of Compliance drafts the narrative.
5. A second person approves the narrative. The maker may not approve their own.
6. The pack is issued. The numbers as at issue are snapshotted, so the
   committee's record is fixed even though the live view moves on.
7. Issuing files the pack as evidence against the committee meeting's task, so
   producing the pack discharges the duty to hold and minute the meeting.

### JRN-05 A duty is going to be late, and that is handled correctly

The journey whose correct outcome is a refusal. FRD §5.14, §5.4.

1. A first-line owner sees a duty they cannot complete inside its window,
   because the estate is frozen for a migration.
2. The owner tries to mark the task done. The system refuses: the policy is
   `evidence` and there is nothing to attach.
3. The owner raises an exception against the duty, with a reason, a
   compensating control and a requested expiry.
4. The owner tries to approve their own exception. The system refuses, naming
   the rule.
5. A Compliance Manager approves it. The exception reads Active and the duty
   stops escalating on the ordinary ladder.
6. Seven days before expiry the exception reads Expiring soon and the owner and
   the approver are both chased.
7. Nobody decides. The exception expires and enters review. It reads as an open
   exposure in the one remediation register and escalates on the standard
   ladder, reaching the executive rung at seven days. No shadow issue is
   created; the expired exception is itself the exposure.
8. The Compliance Manager renews it. The renewal count increments. On the second
   renewal the system refuses the Compliance Manager and requires the Executive.
9. Past the second renewal the exception is reported by name in the Audit
   Committee pack. The firm has to admit the decision it has been making.

### JRN-06 A report reaches the ethics office and a senior person is refused

The second journey whose correct outcome is a refusal. FRD §5.24, §4.12.

1. A report arrives through the anonymous portal. A reference code is issued and
   metadata is stripped at intake. The reporter's identity is not a field.
2. The report is counted for every viewer, and its body opens only for people on
   its access list.
3. The system computes recusals: anyone named in the allegation, and the head of
   the department the allegation points at. Those people cannot open the case,
   whatever their role, and the case never reaches their queue.
4. The head of that department, who holds the Compliance Manager role and sits
   on the ethics office, opens the case. The system refuses, naming recusal.
   Recusal beats clearance.
5. The Head of Internal Audit acknowledges the report inside the acknowledgement
   window. The chasing is pointed at the ethics office, never at the reporter.
6. Triage accepts it and assigns an investigator.
7. The investigator messages the reporter through the reference code without
   learning who they are.
8. Remediation is raised into the one issues register.
9. A second person closes the case with an outcome and substantive feedback,
   owed whether or not the allegation stood up. Closure is refused without both.
10. The audit trail records that the report was triaged and by whom. It never
    repeats the allegation.

### JRN-07 A change in the law reaches the right person before it is urgent

FRD §5.3.

1. A change arrives from the regulatory-intelligence feed with its source and
   its publication date.
2. The system computes the impact set: which obligations, controls and policies
   the change touches.
3. Every impacted record's owner is alerted automatically. Nobody pressed send.
4. A Compliance Analyst reviews the impact and acknowledges the change.
5. The Analyst patches what actually changed: a due date, a requirement, a
   control description, a policy review date.
6. Where the change creates a genuinely new duty rather than amending an
   existing one, the Analyst promotes it into the source pipeline. Editing an
   existing duty to mean something new would destroy the provenance of what it
   used to mean.
7. The change closes. Closure is refused while any impacted record is
   unacknowledged.
8. Where the assessment is that nothing is affected, that is recorded as a
   decision with a basis. No impact is a decision, not an absence.

## 5. Time-driven behaviour

Look for this deliberately. A prototype never has to wait until tomorrow, so
scheduling is where it is thinnest.

**One ladder, and only one.** Reminders to the owner at seven, three and one days
before the due date. Escalation at one day overdue to the owner and their
department head, at three days to the compliance escalation owner, at seven days
to the executive. The intervals are configurable; the existence of the ladder is
not.

**Recurring cycles.** On approval of a cycle, the next instance is created at the
cadence, from the later of this cycle's due date and today, carrying the same
duty, owner, checker and provenance, with cleared evidence and the maker-checker
state reset. Event-based, continuous and daily duties are not auto-scheduled,
because their next occurrence is not a function of the calendar. Generating the
next instance never closes a missed one.

| TIM-21 | Next policy review | A version is published | NOT PRESENT |
| TIM-22 | Next third-party diligence | A diligence campaign task is approved | NOT PRESENT |

| TIM-26 | Monitoring rule run | Its own run frequency | SIMULATED, one current run per rule, seeded |
| TIM-27 | Regulatory source scan | A schedule, or a person starting a run | SIMULATED, scripted proposals |
| TIM-28 | Connector sync | The connector's sync cadence | SIMULATED, statuses and last-sync times are seeded |

**Clocks.** An incident's regulator clocks start at detection, never at record
creation. A clock triggered by a later discovery starts at that discovery, and
the divergence from the detection time is recorded explicitly. Countdowns
animate client side from a fixed deadline and must not poll. A breached clock
stays breached and is never cleared by closing the record.

**Every fired rung is written to the audit log** and surfaced on the record, in
notifications and in the queue. The firm must be able to prove it chased, not
merely assert it. TIM-01 and TIM-02 now do: every duty and every step of a
multi-step duty fires for real, to real people, with a real trail. Every
other line in this section still waits on the table its deadline lives on.
