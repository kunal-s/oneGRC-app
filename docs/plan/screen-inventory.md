Every surface the prototype carries, the module it belongs to, the entity it is
about, and how much of it is real today. Edited in place at every slice close-out.

**Role codes.** `Exec` Executive · `RiskMgr` Risk Manager · `CompMgr` Compliance
Manager · `Analyst` Compliance Analyst · `CtrlOwner` Control Owner · `Auditor`
Auditor · `Admin` Administrator · `AuditCttee` Audit Committee Chair ·
`RiskCttee` Risk Committee Chair. `all` means every one of the nine.
The Roles column is the prototype's own navigation matrix
(`src/components/nav-config.ts`, `NAV_VISIBILITY`), which matches FRD §4.9 row
for row. The Administrator is offered every surface.

**Status values.** `not built` · `scaffolded` · `wired` · `verified`.
In this repo `scaffolded` means the screen exists in `apps/web` exactly as the
client approved it and is driven by the in-memory seed world, so nothing real
flows through it. `wired` means it reads or writes through `apps/api`.

**Route convention.** Where one route hosts several screens, the route cell
carries the qualifier that selects them (`persona:`, `tab:`, `section:`,
`form:`, `view:`, `modal:`). Each route appears in exactly one screen family.
